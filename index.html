Our story

Rooted in Nebbi and West Nile.

Nile Tropical Industries is based on Abindu Road in Nebbi Municipality, in Uganda's West Nile region. Its manufacturing work includes products developed around locally available materials and customer needs.

Shea Butter is an important part of the company's cosmetics and cosmeceuticals work, alongside a wider range of soaps, detergents, foods, beverages, candles and repellents.
