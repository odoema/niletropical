About Nile Tropical Industries

Nile Tropical Industries (U) Ltd is a locally rooted Ugandan manufacturer developing practical products for homes, families, traders and retailers from Nebbi, West Nile.

The enterprise is led by Hon. Dr. Betty Udongo Pacutho. Products include Mozzie-Guard jelly and other household products made for the Ugandan market.

Factory and office: Plot 27 Abindu Road, Olyeko Cell, Abindu Division, Nebbi Municipality.
